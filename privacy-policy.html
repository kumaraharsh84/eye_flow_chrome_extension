Privacy Policy — EyeFlow

Last updated: June 2025

Short version: EyeFlow does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. Everything stays on your device, in your browser's local storage.

What is EyeFlow?

EyeFlow is a Chrome extension that detects doom scrolling on sites like Instagram, YouTube, and Reddit, and interrupts with guided eye break exercises and hydration reminders. It is designed as a personal wellness tool.

What data does EyeFlow collect?

EyeFlow stores the following data — only on your local device, in Chrome's local storage:

  • Your extension settings (reminder intervals, sensitivity, theme)
  • Usage stats (how many eye breaks completed, doom scroll sessions blocked today/this week)
  • Mood responses you tap after eye breaks (e.g. 😌 😐 😤)
  • Scroll pattern data (which sites and time-of-day you scroll most — used only to show you insights)
  • Time spent on tracked sites (used only to calculate reminders)

What data does EyeFlow NOT collect?

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers
  • We do not read the content of pages you visit
  • We do not record what you type
  • We do not send any data to any server
  • We do not share any data with third parties
  • We do not use analytics tools, trackers, or ads

Where is data stored?

All data is stored locally using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API. This means the data lives on your device only. It is not synced across devices and is deleted when you uninstall the extension or clear your browser data.

Why does EyeFlow need host permissions?

EyeFlow runs on a small set of tracked browsing sites only, such as Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, Facebook, X, TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitch, and Snapchat. It does not read page content — it only tracks scroll events and the current URL to determine which reminder mode to use (e.g., doom scroll site vs normal work site).

Why does EyeFlow need the "tabs" permission?

The tabs permission is used to broadcast settings changes to all open tabs when you update your preferences in the popup. It is not used to read tab content or history.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Since no data is collected externally, changes would only ever restrict data use further.

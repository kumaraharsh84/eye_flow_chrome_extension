Privacy Policy — EyeFlow

Last updated: November 2026

Short version: EyeFlow has no servers and collects nothing. Everything stays on your device, in your browser's local storage. The only time data leaves your device is the optional weekly report, which is sent to a webhook address that you enter yourself.

What is EyeFlow?

EyeFlow is a Chrome extension that detects doom scrolling on sites like Instagram, YouTube, and Reddit, and interrupts with guided eye break exercises and hydration reminders. It is designed as a personal wellness tool.

What data does EyeFlow collect?

EyeFlow stores the following data — only on your local device, in Chrome's local storage:

  • Your extension settings (reminder intervals, sensitivity, theme)
  • Usage stats (how many eye breaks completed, doom scroll sessions blocked today/this week)
  • Mood responses you tap after eye breaks (e.g. 😌 😐 😤)
  • Scroll pattern data (which sites and time-of-day you scroll most — used only to show you insights)
  • Time spent on tracked sites (used only to calculate reminders)

What data does EyeFlow NOT collect?

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers
  • We do not read the content of pages you visit
  • We do not record what you type
  • We do not operate any server and receive no data from the extension
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties
  • We do not use analytics tools, trackers, ads, or remote fonts

Optional weekly report (webhook)

This feature is off by default. If you paste an https webhook address (for example a Formspree or Make.com form) into the popup, EyeFlow sends a weekly summary to that address and nowhere else. The summary contains eye-break and water-break counts, minutes spent per tracked site, the peak scrolling time, and a log of recent doom-scroll sessions (site name, start time, duration). It never contains page content, URLs of normal sites, or anything you typed. Chrome asks for your permission for that one address before it is saved. While a webhook is active the popup shows "Locked by Sibling Monitor", and the webhook can only be removed with the PIN shown when it was created. The service that receives the report handles it under its own privacy policy.

Where is data stored?

All data is stored locally using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API. This means the data lives on your device only. It is not synced across devices and is deleted when you uninstall the extension or clear your browser data.

Why does EyeFlow run on websites?

Doom-scroll tracking happens only on a fixed list of sites: Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, Facebook, X, TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitch, and Snapchat. There it looks at the current URL and at scroll, tap, and key activity to tell whether you are actively scrolling a feed. On every other website EyeFlow stays dormant. It runs no timers and sends nothing. It is present only so it can show a gentle eye or water reminder on the page you are looking at, and it checks the page address and form fields first so it stays quiet on banking, sign-in, and meeting pages. Page content is never read, stored, or transmitted.
